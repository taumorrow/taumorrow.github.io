01

Four works, one method

Each is a different game. Each runs on the same idea: write the rules as formulas a machine can decide, let the machine decide them for every case, and keep a record that anyone can recompute.

Taumorrow Chess — a chess engine that cannot bluff

A chess engine whose every move is decided by logic, not estimated by a network. Shortcuts that make the search faster are admitted only with a decided proof that they never change the result. It plays frontier language models live, in public, and shows its own view of the position as it thinks.

What it learns, it learns from its own games, under a gate that is decided before any change is accepted. Games against language models are a measure of strength, never training material.

The Round as a Formula — multiplayer games whose every round you can recompute

Lotteries, shared pots, a hand of cards, a move on a board, games against a bank: games where several parties act at once and one step decides the round. Usually that step is a program run by an operator you have to trust. Here the round is a formula, every party can recompute it from the record, and the record cannot be rewritten.

The method began as a whitepaper in December 2025 and is published as a paper with the executions a third party can repeat.

  • PaperThe Round as a Formuladoi:10.5281/zenodo.23208762 · 7 Oct 2026
  • OriginProvably Fair Multiplayer Gaming via Formal Verificationwhitepaper, December 2025, public repository
  • StatusMethod published. Parts of the method “The Round as a Formula” are patent pending.

AXIOM — a game where the formula is the implementation

A board game for two to four players with simultaneous moves, built in December 2025 as a production demonstration of the method. The dice from all players’ commitments, the collisions, the state transitions and the winner are not computed by a program and then checked: each is a formula in Tau Language, and the formula is the implementation. The services around it only carry messages.

After every round, anyone can download the specification and the recorded inputs and re-synthesize them. The outputs match or the inconsistency shows. This is where the sentence “Tau is the engine, everything else delivers messages” comes from, and where the fair gaming work began.

  • StatusRan in production from December 2025; in maintenance since October 2026. Parts of the method “The Round as a Formula” are patent pending.

A Playable Nomic — the game where changing the rules is the game

Nomic has been played since 1982. Where it has been computerized, a machine executes the rules the players write. Here the machine also decides whether a proposed rule change is legal with respect to the constitution, before it takes effect, and an entrenched core is proven to survive every sequence of legal steps.

Two levels of self-amendment on a decidable substrate: the players change the rules, the rules cannot change what was proven.

02

Taumorrow Games

A series of multiplayer games on the same method: every round is a formula, sealed before the draw, recomputable by anyone. Play money only, non-commercial, 18+. Taumorrow Games exists to demonstrate this method; play money is the means, not the purpose.

Orbit 37 — a wheel with 37 pockets

Four seats play against the bank. Before each draw, every seat and the bank seal a number; a formula in the Tau language adds them modulo 37. Once sealed, no one can change the pocket. The bank can only refuse to open, and then it pays every valid bet in chips, as if it had won, and the game ends.

Every completed round is a signed record that the page and anyone else can recompute. Chips are play money without value: they cannot be bought, sold, transferred or paid out.

Why a wheel first: in a game against the bank, the operator normally holds every card. It draws, it pays, it keeps the record. That is the hardest case for trust, so it is the first case for the method. In Orbit 37 every seat and the bank seal a number before the draw, the pocket is their sum modulo 37, and the bank can only refuse to open. The aim is a game in which the operator cannot change the pocket once the numbers are sealed; the wheel is just the simplest board for it.

03

How it works, in four steps

The same four steps carry all four works. Nothing here is a parameter; the parameters stay in the workshop.

1

Small formulas, each decided

The logic is split by task into small specifications. The engine decides each one for every possible input, so there is no “probably”.

2

Dependencies become wiring

The output of one piece is the input of another. A transport layer passes values along and decides nothing itself.

3

Shortcuts need a proof

Anything that makes a search faster is admitted only with a decided statement that it never changes the result.

4

Every step leaves a record

Each game is a hash-chained record you can recompute. If the machine is wrong, you can show exactly where.

Tau Language, its engine and its logics are the work of Ohad Asor and IDNI AG. The architecture built on it, the four works and the papers are Taumorrow’s own work.
